# Kill round: C07 (tractability argument for moderate credence) -- VERDICT: KILLED
(Condensed record of the refuter's report; snippet-level.)
- Not pre-empted, but against the grain of the literature: tractable-cognition programme (Kwisthout, Wareham, van Rooij 2011; van Rooij et al. 2019), Icard (Phil Sci 2018), and the view that limits favour rounding to certainty (Harman; Staffel, Nous 2019; Kinney, Mind 2025).
- "Exactly when" false: Dagum-Luby give a sufficient condition; bounded treewidth and determinism give tractability with 0/1 entries (Chavira and Darwiche, AIJ 2008); sufficiency-for-necessity fallacy.
- Dagum-Luby's "extreme" = superpolynomial local variance at query/evidence nodes, not philosophers' extremeness; nothing like regularity.
- MAP approximation NP-hard even with probabilities bounded in [l,u] (Abdelbar et al., AIJ 2000); moderation does not secure "can".
- Representation-dependence; worst-case vs instance; conflicts with P(A|A)=1 and conditionalization.
- Lead noted for C08: Lopez-Wild, "Computable Bayesian Epistemology" (PhilSci-Archive 23862).
