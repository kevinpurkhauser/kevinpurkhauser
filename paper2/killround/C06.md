# Kill round: C06 (supervenient but not derivable) -- VERDICT: KILLED
(Condensed record of the refuter's report; 11 searches, snippet-level.)
- "Supervenient yet not deducible" is Broad's classical definition (Kim 2009); Chalmers 2006 defines strong emergence by non-deducibility; Butterfield 2011: emergence compatible with supervenience. The claimed dichotomy is a straw man.
- Undecidability-for-emergence already in Gu, Weedbrook, Perales, Nielsen (Physica D 2009); assessed by Humphreys (2015) and Ippoliti and Caprara (2021); pushed to strong emergence by Murialdo, Cespedes, Cifuentes (PhilSci 25933, 2025).
- Premise (2) conflates algorithmic undecidability, T-relative independence and "not derivable by any proof"; quantifier order reversed (for each T some Hamiltonian is independent of T); stronger theories decide each instance.
- On the a priori entailment notion of the emergence debate the gap is derivable; on T-provability every unprovable arithmetic truth is "emergent" (trivial, level-neutral).
- Thermodynamic-limit, engineered, non-representational models; "actual" overreaches.
- Possible small paper: negative thesis that undecidability results cannot ground strong emergence (T-relativity, quantifier order, level-neutrality), as reply to Murialdo et al. 2025, beyond Humphreys and Ippoliti-Caprara.
