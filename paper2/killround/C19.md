# Kill round: C19 (old evidence as logical learning) -- VERDICT: KILLED
(Condensed record of the refuter's report; snippet-level.)
- Prior art: Garber 1983 (premise 1); Garrabrant et al. 2016 list "use of old evidence" as a desideratum and call their solution weak; Demski (Alignment Forum 2018) argues logical induction makes the problem worse (evidence prices already maximal); Skipper and Bjerring (Erkenntnis 2020); Pettigrew on logical learning.
- Premise (1) false for non-deductive cases (Hartmann and Fitelson 2015; Sprenger 2015; Kinney 2019).
- Biconditional fails both ways: entailment price up without H up (tacking, tautological E); no coherence constraint forces H's price up (Garber's known gap).
- "Computable" verdicts are implementation-relative finite-stage facts with only limit guarantees.
- Mismatch: empirical evidence must be encoded as sentences; no native conditioning.
- Salvage would need a theorem about all logical inductors removing Garber's extrasystematic constraints in the deductive case.
