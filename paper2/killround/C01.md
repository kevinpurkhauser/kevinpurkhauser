# Kill round: C01 (computational underdetermination of subsystem structure) -- VERDICT: KILLED
(Condensed record of the refuter's report; about 11 searches, snippet-level.)
- Premise (2) backwards: the commuting value is attained (GNS on the universal C*-algebra); it is the tensor-product value that may be only a supremum (C_q not closed, Slofstra). NPA non-attainment (arXiv 2510.04943) is a different matter.
- Undecidability concerns uniform procedures over all games; for a fixed game nothing follows.
- Premise (1) itself gives a finite separating Bell functional with gap delta (Hahn-Banach; Cabello, Quintino, Kleinmann, arXiv 2307.02920); finite statistics refute the tensor (closure) model with confidence exponential in N, like any Bell test. Remaining asymmetry between nested hypotheses is standard.
- The real obstacle is mathematical (certifying upper bounds on the tensor value is coRE-hard; explicit separations sought; Lin, MIP^co = coRE, arXiv 2510.07162), not underdetermination by evidence.
- C_q vs C_qa indistinguishability is standard (Scholz and Werner, arXiv 0812.4305).
- Cabello et al. already frame the question as empirical and testable.
- Possible survivor: a thesis about inferential access to a theory's empirical content: whether an experiment bears on subsystem structure depends on mathematical facts (bounds on the tensor value) that no uniform method supplies; evidence is accessible only game by game, where proofs exist. Must engage undecidability-in-physics literature.
