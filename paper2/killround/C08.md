# Kill round: C08 (conditionalization is not computable) -- VERDICT: DAMAGED (borderline KILLED)
(Condensed record of the refuter's report; 11 searches, snippet-level.)
- AFR concerns conditioning on continuous, measure-zero observations (disintegration map); discrete/finite-precision evidence (cylinder sets) conditionalizes computably, so "computable evidence streams" is overstated.
- Classical ratio norm undefined at measure-zero points; Kolmogorov conditionalization (Rescorla) constrains update rules a.e.; Jeffrey: exact real-valued learning is an idealization.
- Uniform map vs individual posteriors conflated; ought-implies-can against ideal norms needs defence.
- Proposed fix circular or identical to AFR's noisy-observation positive theorem.
- Prior art: Lopez-Wild, "Computable Bayesian Epistemology" (PhilSci 23862; Phil Sci 2026); Mercier, Lopez-Wild, Spiegel, arXiv 2606.00363 (2026); Roy (thesis 2011), Freer-Roy-Tenenbaum 2014 interpret AFR as "not computable in general, computable with noise".
- Narrow survivor: for models with exact real-valued evidence (Kolmogorov conditionalization, cognitive-science Bayesian models), no computable rule satisfies Kolmogorov conditionalization a.e.; so noisy updating becomes normatively basic, or computable agents are irrational by those models' lights. Must engage Lopez-Wild.
