REFUTATION REPORT: "Mathematics gates evidence" (kill_S1.md)

Limits on what I checked: arxiv.org and cs.utexas.edu are blocked in this session, so I couldn't fetch any full texts. I used about 10 searches and worked from abstracts and search snippets. Where a claim rests on my own knowledge or reconstruction, I say so.

**(a) Prior work**
- **Cabello, Quintino and Kleinmann, "Logical possibilities for physics after MIP*=RE" (arXiv:2307.02920, 2023).** It already frames the physics question this way. A separating Bell-like inequality exists. Commuting-operator correlations that tensor-product correlations cannot approximate need infinite-dimensional systems. It then sorts four possible universes by whether such tests exist and can be done. The paper's experimental side, "which universe are we in depends on finding and testing a separating inequality", is close to the thesis. What it adds is the epistemology label. I could not verify whether they also say explicitly that finding the inequality is not algorithmic.
- **MIP^co = coRE (arXiv:2510.07162, 2025).** It states outright that no algorithm can find explicit separations between the two models for general nonlocal games. Premise (2), "no uniform method", is therefore already the stated folklore in the technical literature.
- **Older framing.** Scholz and Werner (2008), Fritz (2012), and Navascués, Cooney, Pérez-García and Villanueva (2012, "A physical approach to Tsirelson's problem") all present Tsirelson's problem as the question of whether a separating Bell inequality exists, and discuss its physical meaning.
- **Philosophy.** I found no philosophy paper on MIP*=RE and testability as such. That gap is real, but small. The general thesis, that which evidence bears on a hypothesis depends on a logical or mathematical knowledge state with no uniform completion, is standard:
  - Hacking (1967), "slightly more realistic personal probability".
  - Garber (1983) and the logical-omniscience / old-evidence literature: evidential relevance appears only when a logical fact is learned.
  - Humphreys on epistemic opacity.
  - The classical point that the observational consequence set of a first-order theory is only recursively enumerable, not decidable.
  - The undecidability-in-physics literature, including the Ippoliti and Caprara epistemology paper on Cubitt et al. (J. Gen. Phil. Sci. 2021).

**(b) Premises and inference**

(iv) and (i), the decisive points. An explicit separating game with a proven bound appears to exist.
- Several search snippets of the MIP*=RE paper (arXiv:2001.04383, later version) say it exhibits an explicit game G with val*(G) ≤ 1/2 < val^co(G) = 1.
- A self-referential construction makes this plausible. Use the recursion theorem to build a machine M that runs the NPA hierarchy on its own game G_M and halts if NPA certifies val^co(G_M) < 1.
  - If M halted, then val*(G_M) = 1 ≤ val^co(G_M), so NPA could never certify val^co(G_M) < 1. That contradicts the reason M halted, so M never halts.
  - Since M never halts, val*(G_M) ≤ 1/2.
  - Since NPA converges to val^co, val^co(G_M) < 1 would eventually be certified. It never is, so val^co(G_M) = 1.
- I could not see the full text, so check the exact theorem before relying on this. If it holds, the thesis's central claim fails: "open to evidence only piecemeal, game by game, as proofs arrive". One proven game settles the access question for the binary choice between tensor-product and commuting-operator models, permanently. The obstacle was a one-time mathematical one, already cleared. What remains is physical: the game is astronomically large, and no explicit perfect commuting-operator strategy is given. That is a different and familiar kind of gap.
- Even without explicitness, the inference "no uniform method, therefore access is piecemeal" is invalid for the hypothesis choice. Undecidability across all games implies nothing about whether one good game is available. Testing a hypothesis needs one witness, not a decision procedure.

(ii) Physical realizability.
- Any separating game needs infinite-dimensional strategies whose correlations cannot be approximated in finite dimensions.
- Renner and Wolf ("Commuting operations factorise", 2023) show that commuting operations factorise when the inputs are finite-dimensional.
- In standard algebraic QFT, local algebras are hyperfinite (my knowledge: Buchholz, D'Antoni and Fredenhagen), and the split property holds for strictly spacelike-separated regions. Correlations from hyperfinite algebras lie in the tensor-product closure (a 2025 review notes there are no counterexamples for approximately finite-dimensional algebras).
- So in our best physics the "commuting-operator hypothesis" has no realized excess content. A hypothesis that predicts super-tensor correlations is a hypothesis of non-hyperfinite local physics, which is exotic. The thesis's "real empirical content" therefore sits on a hypothesis that current theory already excludes. This doesn't make the thesis false, but it does make the flagship example nearly idle.

Asymmetry the proposal ignores.
- The commuting-operator set is the larger set. It permits violations but does not predict them.
- Data can refute the tensor-product hypothesis (one observed violation of a proven bound) but can never refute the commuting-operator hypothesis.
- "Evidential relevance to the choice" is one-directional, and its mathematical prerequisite is met by the single proven game above. The other direction, confirming the tensor-product hypothesis by non-violation, is limited by which commuting-operator strategies can physically be implemented, not by missing proofs.

(iii) Difference in kind.
- "No uniform method for certified upper bounds" amounts to this: true bounds of the form val* ≤ 1/2 form a coRE-hard set, so every consistent formal system leaves infinitely many of them unprovable.
- That is Gödel-style incompleteness for Π1 facts, the same structure as an undecidable spectral gap or a theory whose observational consequences are only recursively enumerable.
- It is a sharp instance of a known kind, not a new kind. The "Why surprising" paragraph ("accounts of testability assume we can compute what a hypothesis predicts… here that fails in a principled way") is also false as a claim about the literature. Bayesian work on logical non-omniscience, and the undecidability-in-physics literature, already reject that assumption.

**(c) Trivial or standard?** As epistemology, yes. The content reduces to a combination of known pieces:
- logical non-omniscience (Garber and Hacking);
- uncomputable predictions (Pour-El and Richards; Cubitt, Pérez-García and Wolf);
- the technical folklore that separations cannot be found algorithmically.

The concession, "once a bound is proven, ordinary statistics decide", is exactly the standard old-evidence picture.

**VERDICT: DAMAGED (bordering on KILLED in its stated form)**

**(1) Decisive reasons**
- The "piecemeal, game by game" access claim fails for the hypothesis the thesis names. One proven separating game suffices, and an explicit one with val* ≤ 1/2 and val^co = 1 apparently already exists from the MIP*=RE construction (I could not check the full text). The access limit is therefore a one-time obstacle that has been cleared, not a standing feature.
- "No uniform method" for arbitrary games doesn't carry over to the hypothesis choice, which needs only one witness. The inference is invalid.
- The supposed new access condition is a sharp case of well-studied logical non-omniscience and incompleteness. The "Why surprising" claim misdescribes the literature (Hacking, Garber, Humphreys, the undecidability-in-physics work).
- The commuting-operator alternative's excess content needs non-hyperfinite local algebras, which standard QFT excludes. The example is close to idle, and the asymmetry (only the tensor-product hypothesis can be refuted) is not addressed.

**(2) What it would have to become to survive**
- **Shift from computability to feasibility.** Do separating games exist, with proven bounds, small enough to run and with a gap that tolerates noise? The proven explicit game is astronomically large. If small proven separations are unknown, or provably have to be huge, then evidential access really is gated by the progress of mathematics, at the scale of tests that can actually be run. That would be a resource-bounded, complexity-theoretic access condition. It would need to be argued as distinct from ordinary intractability.
- **Or shift the target hypothesis.** Drop the binary tensor-versus-commuting choice. Use instead fine-grained hypotheses about which correlation set nature actually realises: its shape, and particular facets for particular scenarios. Each facet's relevance would need its own bound proof, so the work really is piecemeal, and the coRE-hardness of upper bounds ensures this never ends.
- **Either version must engage:** Cabello, Quintino and Kleinmann; the logical-omniscience literature; the AQFT hyperfiniteness and split-property point; and the refute-only asymmetry.
