# 01. Open-problem ledger (Phase 1)

Method: four parallel research subagents, each covering three areas (12 areas in total), searched work from roughly 2017-2026 for problems the authors themselves flag as open, and for "importable results" from mathematics, computer science, economics, physics or psychology that bear on a philosophical debate apparently without being cited in it. Full condensed reports: `mining/A_*.md` to `mining/D_*.md`.

Constraint: full-text access was blocked (Phase 0), so every quote is at most snippet-level and must be re-verified in Phase 3. The shared web-search budget (200 searches per turn) ran out during mining; three of four agents stopped short of their quotas.
