## Critique
- Over budget by about 400 words. Background (2) is too long: AFR results can be stated where used.
- The rule-following principle carries the weight but appears only in section 4. The central obstacle (satisfying vs showing, rated 5/5) is postponed to section 6, so the reader meets the main objection after the main argument has been "established". It should be met inside section 4.
- Redundancy: Lemma 4 (noise floor) is both a formal result and an objection reply. Put it once, in section 5.
- Missing: an explicit statement of what the paper does not claim (evaluative ideal untouched; discrete evidence untouched). It should come early, to prevent overclaiming charges.
- Vague: "open-ended learning" is not defined. Define it as learning from a source introduced after the prior was formed and modelled by its relation to the world.
