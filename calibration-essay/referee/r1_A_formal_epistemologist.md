Record of the report (condensed by the author agent; all substantive points retained, wording close to the original).

Verdict: MAJOR REVISION. Scores: originality 7, correctness 5, importance 7.

## Originality
New in the Dunn/Tang reliabilism debate: the schema CR-S(X,W); the point that the modal-robustness reply to Goldman fails because uninformed calibration is not fragile; systematic use of expert-testing results. The Lemma is a routine diagonal construction (Oakes/Dawid in terms of chances); present it as such. The section 7 measure is not novel.

## Core argument
1. Run-level vs world-level: "meets S with probability at least 1 - epsilon" treats S as run-level, but "X-calibrated throughout W(w,rho)" quantifies over other worlds. Formalize; make the standard world-level. Then the core is a single counterexample (the witness is calibrated everywhere, so CR-S says it is reliable at its anti-tracking world, contradicting R). Securability explains the generalization but does no logical work beyond it; say so.
2. MOST SERIOUS: expectation over past coins. The Lemma bounds E|f_t - c_t| unconditionally over coins, but (R) speaks of chance given everything the process takes as input, including its own earlier credences. Example: rho flips a coin on day 1 and then says 0.9 forever or 0.1 forever. Then f-bar = 0.5, chance 0.1 every day; on the 0.1-runs rho matches the chance perfectly, yet the ex ante reading of R calls it unreliable. Untenable. This is exactly the Sandroni random-theory forecaster of section 6. Repairs: (i) let the chance depend on past forecasts (FV tolerates this adversary), but then w_rho is reactive, not exogenous: rewrite 3.2, 5.3, 5.4; (ii) use the deterministic Foster-Hart witness (f-bar = f), but then only smooth calibration is covered.
3. (R) is less weak than claimed: it fixes the reference class as everything the process takes as input, which builds in the indicator view. Accuracy defence establishes R only for someone already measuring reliability by chance-expected accuracy. In w_rho the witness's forecasts hover near 1/2 (calibration forces this); "points the wrong way" overstates. The calibrationist can say (D1) is not idle: truth in w_rho is unpredictable relative to procedures that do not encode its law; 1/2 is minimax-sensible. Needs an argument. The chance-free variant compares with an omniscient process: exactly the Brier-to-truth standard D1 rejects; drop or rebuild. Honest framing: the argument shows calibrationism is incompatible with any chance-given-inputs constraint on reliability, however weak; it does not show calibrationism fails by its own lights.
4. Step 7 false as stated for every family R: (a) process-relative coarse classes {t: f-bar_t < 1/2} and complement: the Lemma itself shows calibration on them is not securable (for deterministic processes this is "days I said less than 1/2"; smooth witness can fail it); (b) all computable checking rules restricted to computable processes: no computable forecaster is calibrated against all computable rules on all sequences; SSV witness for that family not computable; close to Dawid 1985; (c) MOST IMPORTANT OMISSION: cross-world (synchronic) calibration: among nearby worlds sharing history h in which rho gives p, a proportion p have rain. FV guarantees nothing here; in w_rho cross-world frequency at fixed h equals c_t, so the witness is badly miscalibrated cross-world. This may collapse into chance-matching given inputs; then the thesis should be that the only viable calibrationism is a disguised indicator view.
5. Proper-score claims: "every proper score" should be "every strictly proper score"; "dominated" has a technical meaning (accuracy dominance in every world); avoid. Rates: Dagan et al. break T^{2/3}; rates are l1 while A1 is squared; align. Check exact form of FV guarantee (a.s. limsup vs expectation).
6. 5.2: process types do not inherit calibration; natural types (extrapolating from the weather record) include miscalibrated members; the generality problem may help the calibrationist. 5.5 too brief: comparative standards compare realized accuracy, do not aggregate; consider benchmarks from a fixed world-independent class (no-regret, Schurz meta-induction).

## Positive proposal
Measure not novel: Brier decomposition textbook; general case Bregman divergence; chance-expected accuracy already in Pettigrew 2016, 2021, Tang 2016. "Could have avoided given what it used" ambiguous: c_g not available to the process. Ground = all inputs including coins departs from Tang (mental states); generality problem relocated; what is c_g in a deterministic world? "Forced" too strong (comparison, restricted domains, cross-world calibration are alternatives). Humean third point weak (expected credence is dispositional, not in the mosaic); drop or develop. "Symptom" point: credit Dawid 1982.
