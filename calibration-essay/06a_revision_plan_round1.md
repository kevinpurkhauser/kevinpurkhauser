# 06a. Revision plan after referee round 1

All three referees: major revision (scores: A 7/5/7, B 8/5/7, C 7/5/7). Converging objections, and the response adopted:

1. **Coin gap (A2, B-G/H, C 2.5).** The Lemma bounds expectation over all coins; a randomizer can match the chance on many runs. Response: two forms of the Lemma. (a) Broad individuation (the randomized process as a whole): expected distance at least 0.4 every day. (b) Narrow individuation (the process with its coin sequence, or drawn theory, fixed, which is deterministic): for every outcome path and every horizon T, with probability at least 2/7 over coins the average distance over days 1..T is at least 0.2 (Markov-type bound, since distance is at most 0.9). Combined with probability-one (or 1 - eps, eps < 2/7) passing, there are runs on which the realized deterministic process passes and is far from chance on average. The world stays exogenous. Premise R restated in average form. Rhetoric ("every occasion", "points the wrong way") removed; witness described accurately (hovers near 1/2).
2. **Step 7 overgeneralized (A4, B-A/B/J, C 2.8).** Restrict horn 1 to standards with positive tolerance (or smooth at fixed bandwidth), reference families fixed independently of the assessed process and countable or of bounded complexity, finite-horizon only beyond a threshold. Then add horn 2: standards that escape securability: (a) process-relative / propensity-binned classes; (b) computable calibration for computable processes (Dawid 1985); (c) cross-world calibration. New lemma (hidden-structure world, Fubini) shows (a) is insufficient unless processes are restricted to computable ones; (b) escapes, but by Dawid's agreement theorem it amounts in computable-chance worlds to asymptotic agreement with the chances; (c) needs a weighting of worlds, and chance-weighting makes it chance-matching. Thesis becomes a dilemma: securable and insufficient, or unsecurable and (asymptotically) chance-tracking.
3. **R not weak (A3.3, B-D).** Concede R is chance-relative. Defend it via D1 properly understood: in w_rho the chance is a function of the very inputs rho uses, a same-input process matches it, and a Bayesian with w_rho in its support merges with it (Blackwell-Dubins) when rho is computable. Add realized-Brier track-record fact (gap a.s.). Drop chance-free deterministic-world variant.
4. **In-w vs at-w (B-C).** Require only that the range is reflexive at an actual normal world; take w_rho as actual.
5. **Theorem 2 hypotheses (C E2, E3).** OS 2008: future-independent tests rejecting in finite time. Shmaya: truth passes with probability one. Restate (G).
6. **Mischaracterizations (C E4, E5; B-K).** OS 2009b can reject in finite time using predictions about unobserved data. Al-Najjar-Weinstein: identifies the informed expert or else forecasts are close; test is record-based, success guarantee presupposes an informed expert. Dekel-Feinberg: continuum hypothesis for stronger result. Al-Najjar et al. 2010: restriction on admissible theories.
